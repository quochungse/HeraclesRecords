The Strength screen shows your COROS strength sessions on a 3D map of the muscles you trained. If you log your lifting in Hevy, those workouts can join them.

## Connect Hevy

1. Hevy's developer API comes with **Hevy Pro**. In Hevy, open [Settings → Developer](https://hevy.com/settings?developer) and create an API key.
2. In Heracles Records, open **Strength**, press **Settings** and, beside Hevy, **Connect**. With no COROS account signed in, **Connect Hevy** is on the screen itself.
3. Paste the key and save.

Your Hevy workouts appear beside your COROS sessions, and a session recorded in both is merged into one. The key is stored encrypted on this computer and never synced. Once connected, **Manage** beside Hevy in **Settings** opens the same dialog to change the key, choose whether warm-up sets count, or disconnect. **Data source** in the same place chooses COROS, Hevy or both combined.

## Training load

COROS's Training Load is read from heart rate, which counts lifting light. **Training load formula**, in the Strength screen's **Settings**, chooses how strength sessions are counted:

- **COROS TL**: COROS's own figure, as on your watch.
- **Heracles TL**: Heracles Training Load, worked out from each set's weight and reps (and the RPE you log in Hevy), your body weight and the most you have lifted on each exercise, on the same scale as COROS's load for a run. The muscle map gains a **Load** view, and Overview, the Calendar, the Training Library and Activities count strength this way. Coach and AI Plan set a plan's weekly load in it too.

Hevy keeps no training load of its own, so a workout logged only in Hevy has one only with Heracles TL. Other sports keep COROS's load either way. With Heracles TL, Load Impact, Base Fitness, Intensity trend and the recommended weekly load are worked out by COROS's own formulas on it; hover one to see COROS's own figure. Recovery and training effect stay COROS's, as on your watch.

Every step of the formula, the research behind it and a worked example are on [How a strength session is scored](/activities/strength/training-load).

## Good to know

- Hevy is read only on the Strength screen, so keep Strength on the sidebar while Hevy is connected (Settings → Navigation → Sport screens says so if you hide it).
- New and edited workouts arrive the next time Strength refreshes.
