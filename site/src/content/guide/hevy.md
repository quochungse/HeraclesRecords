The Strength screen shows your COROS strength sessions on a 3D map of the muscles you trained. If you log your lifting in Hevy, those workouts can join them.

## Connect Hevy

1. Hevy's developer API comes with **Hevy Pro**. In Hevy, open [Settings → Developer](https://hevy.com/settings?developer) and create an API key.
2. In Heracles Records, open **Strength** and press **Connect Hevy**.
3. Paste the key and save.

Your Hevy workouts appear beside your COROS sessions, and a session recorded in both is merged into one. The key is stored encrypted on this computer and never synced. Once connected, the **Hevy** button on the Strength screen opens the same dialog to change the key, choose whether warm-up sets count, or disconnect.

## Training load

COROS's Training Load is read from heart rate, which counts lifting light. **Training load** on the Strength screen chooses how strength sessions are counted:

- **COROS**: COROS's own figure, as on your watch.
- **Heracles**: Heracles Training Load, worked out from each set's weight and reps, your body weight and the most you have lifted on each exercise, on the same scale as COROS's load for a run. The muscle map gains a **Load** view, and Overview, the Calendar, the Training Library and Activities count strength this way. Hover a figure to see COROS's beside it.

Other sports keep COROS's load either way, and so do the figures COROS works out from it: Base Fitness, Load Impact and the weekly range it recommends. **About Heracles Training Load**, beside the choice, says what your figures are scaled by.

## Good to know

- Hevy is read only on the Strength screen, so keep Strength on the sidebar while Hevy is connected (Settings → Navigation → Sport screens says so if you hide it).
- New and edited workouts arrive the next time Strength refreshes.
