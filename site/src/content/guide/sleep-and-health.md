COROS serves sleep, HRV, stress and daily health through a second door: its MCP server. Until it is connected, the Sleep screen says **Sleep needs MCP**, Stress level and Heart rate have nothing to show and Mentor cannot read your nights.

## Connect it

1. Open **Settings → Connections → MCP Servers → Manage**.
2. Find **COROS** in the list and press **Connect**.
3. Your browser opens COROS's own sign-in page, on your account's region, with your email filled in. Type your password there once and allow access.

That is all. The app reads your nights from then on, and Mentor can read them too.

## What you get

- **Every night and nap**: stages, time asleep, the sleep window and wake-ups.
- **Across the night**: heart rate, HRV and stress.
- **By day**: steps, exercise minutes and stress by the hour, which Mentor reads beside your training load.
- **Stress level** and **Heart rate**: two screens of their own, with Sleep under Health in the sidebar. Your stress every five minutes and the time in each stress level; your resting heart rate, your heart rate asleep and your exercise time in each heart-rate zone.

## Stress level and Heart rate

Both screens open on today, with the last 30 days under **Recent days**. Pick a day to read it.

- **Stress level** draws COROS's reading every five minutes over a line of the day's sleep and sessions, with the day's **Peak** and its time at **High stress** and **Relaxed**. COROS reads stress only while you are still, so the line is dotted through your sessions and the hours the watch was off your wrist. Point at the bar under it to see each level's range and time. **Stress by day** below shows the hours in each level over 4 weeks, 3 months or all of your history.
- **Heart rate** sets the day's resting heart rate against the 7 days before and shows where the day sat on one scale: the whole day from its lowest to its highest, asleep, and each session from its average to its highest. Press a session under **Sessions** to see its own time in each heart-rate zone. **Daily heart rate** below follows your resting rate and range over the same periods, and **Exercise heart rate** adds up your training time in each of your own zones.
- **Today states no average until it is over.** At nine in the morning the day's average is mostly the night's, so today leads with the latest stress reading and the resting heart rate, which the night sets.

The first read brings in a year of days. After that the app asks only for what is new, and for today and yesterday, which the watch may still be syncing.

## Kept past nine weeks

COROS keeps about nine weeks of sleep. Heracles Records keeps every night it has read on your computer, so the longer you use it, the longer your history. Cerberus, the sleep labour in the Hall of Records, counts nights from that history.

## If it stops working

A server that is connected but does not answer shows as **COROS MCP unreachable**. That is COROS's side or your connection, not a setting: try again later. Disconnecting and connecting again in MCP Servers is the fix for a sign-in COROS no longer accepts.

## Other servers

MCP Servers also offers **Strava** and **Freddy** under Quick connect, and lets you add a server of your own. Mentor can use their tools in a conversation. A server other than COROS is withheld from any conversation that switches off one of its sources, since the app cannot tell what that server reads.
