Strength figure assets
======================

The muscle, connective-tissue and skeletal geometry in hero/ is derived from
Z-Anatomy (https://www.z-anatomy.com/), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. Z-Anatomy
includes material derived from BodyParts3D, copyright The Database Center for
Life Science (DBCLS), also licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. The models were exported from
the upstream Blender atlas at revision b16b4c2eb7de824722f5eef9d5f56c5569a3d640
and then reshaped and baked for the Strength view. These derivatives are
distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The heads are built from "Stylized Anime Female Head" by Rodesqa
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/stylized-anime-female-head-e8d04325a74e46a98a6494abe721a8b2),
licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); the head and
face only, decimated, uncoloured, the eyes smoothed, made less stylised, and
reshaped for the man.

The woman's bust is shaped from "female muscle human body" by mina
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/female-muscle-human-body-aa022f8c27e04aea89e731f66484a299),
licensed CC BY 4.0, rescaled and fitted to the figure.
