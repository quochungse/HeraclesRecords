<details>
<summary><strong>First launch</strong></summary>

- **macOS**: this build is not notarized yet. If macOS says it cannot check the app, open **System Settings → Privacy &amp; Security** and click **Open Anyway**. If it says the app is damaged, run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Heracles Records.app"` in Terminal, then open it again.
- **Windows**: SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognised app. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
- **Linux**: make the AppImage executable (`chmod +x HeraclesRecords-*.AppImage`) and run it, or install the deb on Debian / Ubuntu (`sudo apt install ./HeraclesRecords-*.deb`).

You sign in with your COROS account. Mentor also needs an AI: your ChatGPT account, a Claude subscription, a Claude or OpenRouter API key, or a model running on your own computer.

</details>

## Your data stays yours

- Your training comes from your COROS account and stays on your computer. The app has no account of its own and the developer runs no servers.
- Back up everything to one file you keep, and restore it whenever you like.

## The story behind Heracles Records

Heracles Records began as a fork of CorosLink, a desktop companion for COROS watches. I wanted a full record of my training, analytics deeper than a summary screen and an AI mentor that reads all of it, so I rebuilt the app around that. If you are looking for music on your watch, offline maps, watch faces or gear tracking, **CorosLink** does those well and is the app to use.
